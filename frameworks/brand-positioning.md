# Brand Positioning Framework

Use this to get clear on where your brand stands before you design anything. Answer in plain, simple words. If an answer feels fancy, simplify it.

## 1. Who are you for?

Describe one real person, not a "target market".

- Who are they?
- What do they struggle with?
- What languages do they use day to day, at home and at work?

## 2. What do you do for them?

Finish this sentence:

"We help ______ do ______ so they can ______."

## 3. Why you and not someone else?

- What do you do differently?
- What can you say that others can't?
- What do you care about that your competitors don't?

## 4. What's your one big idea?

Boil your brand down to one line. If you can't say it in one line, it's not clear yet.

## 5. Your positioning statement

For [your person] who [their need], [brand name] is a [what you are] that [your main benefit], because [your reason to believe].

## 6. The multilingual check

Your brand will show up in more than one language. Test it.

- Does your core idea still make sense in each language, or does it only work in English?
- Is there a word or phrase in Arabic or Hindi that says it better than English can?
- Does your name sound right and mean the right thing in every language you use?
- Would your audience feel seen, or like you just translated a Western brand?

## 7. Gut check

Read your answers out loud. Does it sound like you? Does your ideal customer nod when they hear it? If not, go back to question 1.
