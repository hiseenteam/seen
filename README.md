# Seen

Open brand strategy frameworks for brands that live in more than one language.

Seen (سين / सीन) is a brand strategy project built for founders and small businesses in India and the Middle East. Most branding resources are made for English-only, Western brands. If your brand speaks Arabic, Hindi and English, you usually get a generic identity, or a logo where the scripts just don't sit well together. Seen is my attempt to fix that.

## Status

Early stage. I'm building this in public, one framework at a time. Nothing here is final, and I'll keep improving it.

## What's inside

- `frameworks/brand-positioning.md`: a step-by-step template to work out where your brand stands
- `frameworks/tone-of-voice.md`: a template to define how your brand sounds in each language

More coming: multilingual logo guidelines, messaging templates, and a guided tool that walks founders through all of it.

## Who this is for

- Founders and small business owners in India, the UAE, KSA and beyond
- Creators and freelancers building a personal brand
- Designers working with Arabic, Devanagari and Latin scripts together

## How to use it

Pick a framework, copy it, and fill it in for your own brand. Everything is free to use and adapt.

## Contributing

Ideas, corrections and new frameworks are welcome. Open an issue or send a pull request. If you work with Arabic or Hindi branding, your feedback is especially useful.
